# Korea symbol market review contract

`data/latest_korea_symbol_market_review.json` connects the five official KRX
market observations to confirmed price, SMA20 and investor-flow facts for the
currently staged Korean pipeline subjects.

This packet is a read-only review bridge. `5/5` means all five observations are
present; it does not mean the Korea regime is classified. The packet never
creates a buy, hold, exit, quantity, order draft, broker submission or real
capital action. Private KIS positions remain outside this public evidence and
are joined only inside the authenticated Portal.
